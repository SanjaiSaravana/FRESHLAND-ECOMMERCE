Description

Pineapple is a tropical fruit with a spiky, rough exterior and sweet, tangy yellow flesh. Native to South America, it is now grown in tropical regions worldwide. Rich in vitamins C and B6, pineapple is enjoyed fresh or used in juices, desserts, and savory dishes for its refreshing taste and health benefits.
