Description

Blueberry is a small, round fruit that is deep blue to purple in color, known for its sweet-tart flavor. Native to North America, blueberries are now cultivated in various regions around the world. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins C and K, and dietary fiber, blueberries are popular for fresh consumption, in baked goods, smoothies, and as a nutritious addition to salads and breakfast dishes.
