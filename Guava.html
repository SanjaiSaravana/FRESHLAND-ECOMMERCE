Description

Guava is a tropical fruit known for its sweet, slightly tangy flavor and rich aroma. It has a green or yellow skin, with pink or white flesh inside, filled with small, edible seeds. Guavas are highly nutritious, packed with vitamin C, fiber, and antioxidants, making them both delicious and healthy.
