Description

Pomegranate is a nutrient-rich fruit with a thick, leathery red skin and a cluster of juicy, jewel-like seeds called arils inside. Native to the Middle East, it is now grown in many warm climates around the world. Pomegranates are high in antioxidants, vitamins C and K, and are prized for their sweet-tart flavor, commonly used in juices, salads, sauces, and desserts.
