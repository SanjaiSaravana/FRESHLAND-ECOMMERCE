Description

Full chicken refers to the entire bird, typically cleaned and prepared for cooking, including both white and dark meat along with bones and skin. It offers a variety of textures and flavors, from the tender breast to the juicy thighs and drumsticks. Full chickens are widely used in global cuisines and can be roasted, grilled, boiled, or used in hearty dishes like biryanis and stews. Rich in high-quality protein, B vitamins, iron, and selenium, whole chicken provides a balanced source of nutrition while delivering savory taste in every bite.
