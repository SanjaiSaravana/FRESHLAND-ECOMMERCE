Description

The mango is a tropical fruit known for its juicy, sweet flavor and vibrant orange-yellow flesh. It belongs to the Mangifera genus and is native to South Asia but is now grown in various tropical regions worldwide. Rich in vitamins A and C, mangoes are enjoyed fresh or used in a variety of culinary dishes, from salads to desserts.
