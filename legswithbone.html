Description

Chicken leg with bone is a tender and flavorful cut of poultry that includes the drumstick and thigh, held together by the bone. With its juicy meat and rich taste, it's a favorite in many cuisines around the world. Often roasted, grilled, fried, or stewed, the bone helps retain moisture and enhances the depth of flavor during cooking. Chicken legs are a good source of protein, B vitamins, and essential minerals like zinc and iron, making them both nutritious and satisfying.
