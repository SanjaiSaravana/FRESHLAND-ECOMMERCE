Description

Banana is a tropical fruit with a soft, sweet flesh encased in a smooth, yellow peel. Native to Southeast Asia, bananas are now cultivated in many tropical and subtropical regions worldwide. Rich in potassium, fiber, and vitamins B6 and C, bananas are commonly eaten fresh, added to smoothies, or used in a variety of baked goods and desserts.
