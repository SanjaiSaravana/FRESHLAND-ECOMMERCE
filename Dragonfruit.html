Description

Dragon fruit, also known as pitaya, is an exotic fruit with a vibrant pink or yellow skin and white or red flesh speckled with tiny black seeds. Native to Central and South America, it is now cultivated in tropical and subtropical regions around the world. Known for its mildly sweet flavor and rich nutrient profile, dragon fruit is high in antioxidants, fiber, and vitamin C, making it a popular choice for fresh consumption, smoothies, and salads.
