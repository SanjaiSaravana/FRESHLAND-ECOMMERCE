Description

Chicken breast boneless is a lean, tender cut of poultry taken from the chest of the bird, with the bone and skin removed. Mild in flavor and low in fat, it's a popular choice for health-conscious meals and is easy to cook by grilling, baking, sautéing, or boiling. Rich in high-quality protein, vitamin B6, and niacin, boneless chicken breast supports muscle growth and energy metabolism. Its versatility makes it ideal for a variety of dishes—from salads and sandwiches to curries and stir-fries.
