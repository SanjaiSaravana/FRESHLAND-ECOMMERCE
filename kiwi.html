Description

Kiwi is a small, nutrient-dense fruit with a fuzzy brown skin and bright green flesh, known for its sweet-tart flavor and tiny edible seeds. Native to China, kiwi is now widely cultivated in countries like New Zealand and Italy. It is rich in vitamin C, fiber, and antioxidants, making it a popular choice for both fresh consumption and use in smoothies, salads, and desserts.
