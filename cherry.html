Description

Cherry is a small, round fruit that comes in various colors, including red, yellow, and black, with a sweet or tart flavor depending on the variety. Native to Europe and Asia, cherries are now cultivated in many temperate regions around the world. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins A and C, cherries are enjoyed fresh, used in desserts like pies and tarts, and can also be made into jams and beverages.
