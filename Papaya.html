Description

Papaya is a tropical fruit with a soft, orange flesh and sweet, mildly musky flavor. Native to Central America, it is cultivated in warm climates around the world. Rich in vitamins A and C, papaya is known for its digestive benefits due to the enzyme papain and is enjoyed both fresh and in a variety of dishes like salads, smoothies, and desserts.
