Description

Grapes are small, juicy fruits that grow in clusters and come in various colors, including green, red, and purple. Native to the Mediterranean region, grapes are cultivated worldwide for fresh consumption, dried as raisins, or fermented to produce wine. They are rich in antioxidants, vitamins C and K, and provide numerous health benefits, from heart health to anti-aging properties.
